# Transparent

**Transparency** is an often needed property for components. However, most components do not support transparency by default. 

Transparancy can be enabled for the following components by means of the property named "Transparent": 

  * [TImage](<TImage.md> "TImage")
  * [TStaticText](<TStaticText.md> "TStaticText")
