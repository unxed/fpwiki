# alias

The [modifier](<modifier.md> "modifier") `alias` gives the opportunity to specify an [assembler](<Assembly_language.md> "Assembly language") name of a [routine](<Routine.md> "Routine"). It is considered deprecated since FPC 2.2.4. Use the [`[public name '…']` modifier](</index.php?title=public&action=edit&redlink=1> "public \(page does not exist\)") instead.
