# NBS Pascal

│ **English (en)** │

The **NBS Pascal Compiler** was a two-pass [Pascal](<Pascal.md> "Pascal") language compiler for the PDP-11 series of computers. It was developed for the U.S. **National Bureau of Standards** (now the **National Institutes of Science and Technology**).
