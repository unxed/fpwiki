# Pi

π (greek letter pi) is the ratio of the circumference of a circle to its diameter. π is about `3.14159265358979`. A full circle has `2*pi()` [radians](<Radian.md> "Radian"). 

In [Free Pascal's](<Free_Pascal.md> "Free Pascal") [RTL](<RTL.md> "RTL"), `system.pi` is a [`function`](<Function.md> "Function") which returns the value of π. This allows you to assign `addr(system.pi)` to a procedural variable. 

`pi()/4` is the same as `arctan(1)`.
