# به ویکی لازاروس و Free Pascal خوش آمدید

## درباره این ویکی

هدف این ویکی به وجود آوردن یک پایگاه اطلاعاتی در مورد لازاروس و [پروژه‌های مرتبط به آن](</index.php?title=%D9%BE%D8%B1%D9%88%DA%98%D9%87%E2%80%8C%D9%87%D8%A7%DB%8C_%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AA%D8%A8%D8%B7&action=edit&redlink=1> "پروژه‌های مرتبط \(page does not exist\)") است. 

FPC already has good user documentation in several formats, but [FPC developer information, organizational information](<FPC_development.md> "FPC development") and subjects not yet [properly documented](<FPC_documentation.md> "FPC documentation") are collected in this wiki. 

Lazarus, on the other hand, has some gaps in the user documentation. So this area was created as an "open document" or "wiki" where anyone can edit and add content. 

The wiki speeds things by making it possible to make changes and additions with a browser! For tutorials, please take a look at the [30 Second Quick Wiki Tutorial](<http://www.chat11.com/30_Second_Quick_Wiki_Tutorial>) or the [WikiPedia Tutorial](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Tutorial>). A [Sand Box](<Sand_Box.md> "Sand Box") is available for practice. If you have any problems, please notify the site [administrator](<http://sourceforge.net/users/vlx/>) or post a bug report on the [Lazarus-CCR](<http://sourceforge.net/projects/lazarus-ccr>) SF site. You can also leave a note or suggestion on our [Site Feedback](<Site_Feedback.md> "Site Feedback") page. 

For the Lazarus **project history** , see the [History](<History.md> "History") page. 

## مستند‌سازی Free Pascal

[مستند‌سازی FPC](</index.php?title=%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%86%D8%AF%E2%80%8C%D8%B3%D8%A7%D8%B2%DB%8C_FPC&action=edit&redlink=1> "مستند‌سازی FPC \(page does not exist\)")
    Contains available documentation for developers and other contributors of the Free Pascal Compiler, such as people involved in translation of messages to other languages, a list of pascal bindings, organizational information, procedure for FPC releases, ToDo lists, etc.

## مستند‌سازی لازاروس

[Lazarus Documentation](<Lazarus_Documentation.md> "Lazarus Documentation")
    All the available documentation and [tutorials](<Lazarus_Documentation.md> "Lazarus Documentation") for the Lazarus IDE (including the Free Pascal Compiler manuals) can be found on the [Lazarus Documentation](<Lazarus_Documentation.md> "Lazarus Documentation") page. Many of the pages are "works in progress" so please feel free to add your experience to these sections. You can also create a personal page with contact information, if you wish.

## دانلود

All currently available components and packages are available on the [Lazarus-CCR Sourceforge files area](<http://sourceforge.net/project/showfiles.php?group_id=92177>). The Free Pascal Compiler and current test versions of Lazarus can be found at [The Lazarus Sourceforge site](<http://sourceforge.net/project/showfiles.php?group_id=89339>). Additional documentation on these packages can also be found on the [Components and Code examples](<Components_and_Code_examples.md> "Components and Code examples") wiki page if the person who contributed the code created one there. The Compiler and a lot of contributed code can be found on the [Free Pascal Compiler download mirrors](<http://www.freepascal.org/download.var>) and on the [Lazarus IDE website](<http://lazarus.freepascal.org>). Lazarus Snapshots download locations can be found [ here](<Lazarus_Snapshots_Downloads.md> "Lazarus Snapshots Downloads"). 

## آدرس‌ ایمیل‌ها

New and experienced users alike are invited to join the Lazarus-ccr [announce](<http://lists.sourceforge.net/lists/listinfo/lazarus-ccr-announce>) and [general](<http://lists.sourceforge.net/lists/listinfo/lazarus-ccr-general>) mailing lists. 

Perhaps even more useful are the [Lazarus](<http://www.lazarus.freepascal.org/modules.php?op=modload&name=StaticPage&file=index&sURL=maill>) IDE mailing list and the [FreePascal](<http://www.freepascal.org/maillist.html>) developers or announcement mailing lists. 

## چه کسی چه کاری می‌کند؟

اگر در حال برگردان اجزا (Component) یا کتابخانه‎ای (Library)هستید ،یا اگر نیاز به برگردان چیزی دارید، لطفا در مورد آن برای ما در صفحه‎ی [Current conversion projects](<Current_conversion_projects.md> "Current conversion projects")
    
    
    یادداشت بگذارید
    

این یادداشت‎ها به سازندگانی که قصد برگرداندن کامپوننت‎ها را دارند 
    
    
    در فهم این که چه چیزی بیشتر مورد نیاز است یاری خواهد کرد
    

و نیز از برگرداندن یک کامپوننت به وسیله‎ی دو شخص مختلف به صورت همزمان جلوگیری می‎کند 

## مراجع، پیوند‌ها و منابع

### محیط توسعه لازاروس (Lazarus IDE)

News and information about the status of the Lazarus IDE can be found on the [Lazarus IDE website](<http://lazarus.freepascal.org>). Ideas, todos, and other plans can be found at [Lazarus Development Process‎](<Lazarus_Development_Process.md> "Lazarus Development Process") page. Releases of the Lazarus IDE and FreePascal compiler can be downloaded from there. The [Lazarus file area](<http://sourceforge.net/project/showfiles.php?group_id=89339>) also has the latest packages of the compiler and IDE available for download. 

#### پروژه‌های فعال لازاروس

Our current listing of [Projects using Lazarus](<Projects_using_Lazarus.md> "Projects using Lazarus") with publicly accessible websites and Lazarus related files to download. 

#### سایت‎های عالی و بخش پیوندها

سایت‎های مرتبط با مجموعه‎ی ما(فری‎پاسکال/لازاروس) و دلفی / کایلیکس را می‎توانید در صفحه‎ی [سایت‎های کد ما](<Page_Of_Code_Sites.md>) پیدا کنید. لطفا اگر سایت خوب دیگری را سراغ دارید که هنوز در لیست وجود ندارد به ما گزارش دهید و یا خودتان دست به کار شده و آنرا به لیست اضافه نمایید. 

#### Specialized Search Engines

There are some outstanding search and knowledge bases online that can be a great help for learning new techniques and solving problems. Tamarack Associates operates a fast [search](<http://www.tamaracka.com/search.htm>) engine specifically for the Borland usenet archives. Mer Systems Inc. provides a similar search [engine](<http://www.mers.com/searchsite.html>). Another outstanding source of information along with a sitewide [search](<http://www.efg2.com/Lab/search.htm>) capability is Earl F. Glynn's Computer Lab and Reference [Library](<http://www.efg2.com/>). 

#### Marketing Case Studies

An [opportunity for Lazarus users](<Marketing_Case_Studies.md> "Marketing Case Studies") to explain why Lazarus is the IDE of choice. 

#### Presenting FPC/Lazarus at a trade show and at contests

FPC/Lazarus was presented with a booth at the [Systems 2005](<Systems_2005.md> "Systems 2005"), the [Systems 2006](<Systems_2006.md> "Systems 2006") and the [Systems 2007](<Systems_2007.md> "Systems 2007") in Munich. Based on the experiences from these trade shows, a collection of [information](<Preparing_a_booth_on_a_trade_show.md> "Preparing a booth on a trade show") to help future preparations for a trade show booth was built. Lazarus and related projects have also participated in a number of Contests, and a [wiki page](<Contests.md> "Contests") was prepared to help future contests inscriptions.
