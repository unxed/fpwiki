## Overview

This page is supposed to provide information and support resources for FPC and Lazarus release process. Note that this page is targeted at the release process as such rather than information about features available in existing releases, [roadmaps for future releases](<Road_map.md> "Road map") showing capabilities expected in future releases or [todo lists](<To_Do_lists.md> "To Do lists") showing steps towards fulfilling the plans for individual future releases.
