# case-sensitive

│ **English (en)** │

Case-sensitive is ability to distinguish between uppercase (capital) and lowercase (small) letters. [Pascal](<Pascal.md> "Pascal") is not case-sensitive (case-insensitive). 

Description  | Case-sensitive  | Case-insensitive   
---|---|---  
Compare two strings, ignoring accents characters  | [`AnsiCompareStr`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansicomparestr.html>) | [`AnsiCompareText`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansicomparetext.html>)  
Compare two null-terminated strings  | [`AnsiStrComp`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansistrcomp.html>) | [`AnsiStrIComp`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansistricomp.html>)  
Compare a limited number of characters of two null-terminated strings  | [`AnsiStrLComp`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansistrlcomp.html>) | [`AnsiStrLIComp`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/ansistrlicomp.html>)  
Compare two ansistrings, ignoring special characters  | [`CompareStr`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/comparestr.html>) | [`CompareText`](<https://www.freepascal.org/docs-html/rtl/sysutils/comparetext.html>)
