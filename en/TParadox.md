![Light bulb](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d8/Nuvola_apps_ktip.png) **Note:** Paradox uses the **BDE** (Borland Database Engine) that is unmaintained and unsupported since around 2000.

**TParadox** [![tparadox.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/f/f4/tparadox.png)](</File:tparadox.png>) is a [TDataSet](<TDataSet.md> "TDataSet") descendant component that provides an interface to Paradox databases. It is available from the [Data Access tab](<Data_Access_tab.md> "Data Access tab") of the [Component Palette](<Component_Palette.md> "Component Palette") as soon as the lazparadox package is installed. 

Most likely you also need `pxlib.dll` and `iconv.dll` available during execution (for Windows users, pre-built dlls are ready for download at <https://sourceforge.net/projects/pxlib/files/pxlib/0.6.3/pxlib-0.6.3-win32.tar.gz/download> and <ftp://ftp.gnupg.org/gcrypt/binary/libiconv-1.9.1.dll.zip>)
