# UTF-8 subscripts and superscripts

  * Basic Latin 0000–007F is identical to [ASCII](<ASCII.md> "ASCII")
  * Superscript one(¹), two (²) and three (³): See [UTF-8_Latin_characters](<UTF-8_Latin_characters.md> "UTF-8 Latin characters")
