This page is a collection of bindings, translated headers and other interfaces that allow Pascal programs to access external libraries. Some of these are available on the Free Pascal Packages, and others on external projects. 

## list of external bindings

  * [ALSA](<https://sourceforge.net/projects/fpalsa/>) (advanced Linux sound architecture)
  * [Cocoa](<FPC_PasCocoa.md> "FPC PasCocoa")
  * [DirectX](<FPC_and_DirectX.md> "FPC and DirectX")
  * [Qt](<FPC_and_Qt.md> "FPC and Qt")
  * [SDL](<FPC_and_SDL.md> "FPC and SDL") (simple direct media layer)



## list of bindings in FPC packages

  * [Apache](<FPC_and_Apache_Modules.md> "FPC and Apache Modules") (modules for the webserver)
  * [Carbon](<FPC_and_Carbon.md> "FPC and Carbon")
  * [DBus](<FPC_and_DBus.md> "FPC and DBus")
  * [ImageMagick](<PascalMagick.md> "PascalMagick")
