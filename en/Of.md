` of` is a [reserved word](<Reserved_word.md> "Reserved word"). 

## occurences

The word `of` is used at several places. 

  * separating the base type of a structured data type 
    * [`array`](<Array.md> "Array") `of basetype`
    * [`file`](</File> "File") `of basetype`
    * [`set`](<Set.md> "Set") `of basetype`
  * in conjunction with the word [`case`](<Case.md> "Case") distinguishing between alternatives 
    * switch control structure: `case of end`
    * variant records: [`record`](<Case.md> "Case") `case of end`
  * specifying class references: [`class`](<Class.md> "Class") `of`
  * specifying references to class [methods](<Method.md> "Method"): [`procedure`](<Procedure.md> "Procedure") `of`
