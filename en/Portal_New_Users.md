Portal:New Users

[![NewUserLogo.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/4/41/NewUserLogo.png)](</File:NewUserLogo.png>) This portal is dedicated to new users of Free Pascal and Lazarus. It is intended to be the place that new users first encounter to learn about Free Pascal and Lazarus. The topics and tutorials on this page should be simple, to the point and aimed at someone who has not previously used Free Pascal and Lazarus.
