# External maintainers

### From Lazarus-ccr

Some platforms or builds in particular formats are not supported directly by active members of the FPC core team due to lack of time or appropriate configuration, but they can be possibly still included in new releases if the build is provided by somebody else. The following contacts are available at the moment:
