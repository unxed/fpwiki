The `do` keyword is a [reserved word](<Reserved_word.md> "Reserved word") that is only used in conjunction with other [keywords](<Keyword.md> "Keyword"). 

## occurences

The keyword `do` usually separates a language construct from an associated (compound) [statement](<statement.md> "statement"). 

  * loops 
    * [`for`](<For.md> "For") … `to` … `do`
    * [`while`](<While.md> "While") … `do`
  * [identifier](<Identifier.md> "Identifier") scope lookup routing: [`with`](<With.md> "With") … `do`
  * [exception](<Exceptions.md> "Exceptions") handling: [`try`](<Try.md> "Try") … [`except`](<Except.md> "Except") `on` … `do`
  * in [extended Pascal](<Extended_Pascal.md> "Extended Pascal") the start of initialization and finalization parts of a module: `to begin do …;` and `to end do …;`
