# Protected

│ **English (en)** │

  
Back to [Reserved words](<Reserved_words.md> "Reserved words"). 

  
The [modifier](<modifier.md> "modifier") `protected` is part of [object-oriented programming](<object-oriented_programming.md> "object-oriented programming"). Within a [`class`](<Class.md> "Class"), a `protected`modifier refers to an area that is restricted to its own `class` or the [methods](<Method.md> "Method") derived from it see and access them.
