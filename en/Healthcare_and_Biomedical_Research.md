## Biomedical Cybernetics

  * A measure of association between vectors based on “similarity covariance”. [Paper](<http://www.zora.uzh.ch/81991/1/1301.4291.pdf>) and [Software](<https://sites.google.com/site/pascualmarqui/home/similaritycovariance>)
  * [SimThyr](<http://simthyr.sourceforge.net>): A numerical simulation program for thyroid homeostasis. 
  * [SPINA](<http://spina.medical-cybernetics.de/en/>): Software for calculating constant structure-parameters of endocrine feedback control systems. 
  * [CyberUnits](<CyberUnits.md> "CyberUnits"): A cross-platform class library for rapid programming of high-performance computer simulations in life sciences. 



## Radiology

  * [MRIcroGL](<http://www.mccauslandcenter.sc.edu/mricrogl/>): Hardware-accelerated renderer for display of 3D medical images. 
  * [MRIcron](<http://www.mccauslandcenter.sc.edu/mricro/mricron/>): Visualizer and volume renderer for medical images (MRI, CT, PET) that also supports statistical analysis and conversion from DICOM format to NIfTI format. 



## Signal Processing

  * [Dr. Sagura Royal Medical Systems AG](<http://www.sagura.com>): Software for medical EEG/PSG Data Acquisition Amplifier Systems. 
  * [ELEcro](<http://www.mccauslandcenter.sc.edu/CRNL/tools/elecro>), a tool for viewing electrophysiological data. 
  * [fMRI Simulator](<http://www.mccauslandcenter.sc.edu/CRNL/tools/fmrisim>), an app that helps design efficient fMRI studies, and demonstrates the principles of fMRI signals. 
  * [Nest-o-Patch](<https://sourceforge.net/projects/nestopatch>), software for the analysis of patch-clamp. two-electrode-voltage clamp and other electrophysiological data. 



## Algorithms, Packages and Units

  * [Laz DICOM Decoder](<http://sourceforge.net/projects/lazdicomdecoder/>), a DICOM decoder class for Lazarus 
  * [PUMA Repository](<PUMA_Repository.md> "PUMA Repository") (Pascal Units for Medical Applications) is a collection of reusable Pascal code modules for health informatics (ncluding an HL7 engine and a converter for measurement units). 
  * [QUANTUM SALIS](<http://quantum-salis.sourceforge.net>), statistical algorithms for life sciences.
