Portal:Hardware and Robotics

[![rpi with adafruit prototyping plate.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/9/98/rpi_with_adafruit_prototyping_plate.png)](</File:rpi_with_adafruit_prototyping_plate.png>) Applications of technical cybernetics include process control, feedback systems technology, measuring and instrumentation, embedded systems, automation, robotics, control of autonomous vehicles, avionics, internet of things and control of hardware components. Lazarus and Free Pascal have been successfully used in an increasing number of projects in this field of continuously growing importance. This portal summarizes articles on real world and demo implementations, algorithms and software projects that have been implemented with Lazarus and/or FPC.
