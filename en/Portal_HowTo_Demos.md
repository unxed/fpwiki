Portal:HowTo Demos

[![HowToLogo.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/7/7b/HowToLogo.png)](</File:HowToLogo.png>) This portal collects together in one place a significant number of short demonstration programs that have been posted on the [Free Pascal and Lazarus Forums](<https://forum.lazarus.freepascal.org/>). Each of the demonstration programs generally provides a simple example of one particular development technique. The Portal was the brainchild of [Handoko](<https://forum.lazarus.freepascal.org/index.php/topic,48264.0.html>) who also provided the initial list of demonstration programs from the Forums.
