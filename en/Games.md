## Pascal Ported Games

This list contains games written in other languages ported to Pascal. Note that most them were written for [Delphi](<Delphi.md> "Delphi") but may be they can be compiled/ported to Free Pascal too. 

Name  | Site  | License  | Notes   
---|---|---|---  
Delphi Doom  | [Sourceforge](<http://sourceforge.net/projects/delphidoom/>) | [GNU General Public License](<https://opensource.org/licenses/gpl-license>) |   
Quake to Delphi  | [Sourceforge](<http://sourceforge.net/projects/delphiquake/>) [Geocities](<http://www.geocities.ws/jimmyvalavanis/applications/delphiquake.html>) | [GNU General Public License](<https://opensource.org/licenses/gpl-license>) | Sourcefoge is the active one. The old-site appears to be quite dead now. Though the source code can still be downloaded.   
Quake II Delphi (Quake 2 Delphi)  | [www.sulaco.co.za](<http://www.sulaco.co.za/quake2/>) | [GNU General Public License](<https://opensource.org/licenses/gpl-license>) |   
C-evo  | [www.c-evo.org](<http://www.c-evo.org/>) | Public Domain  | The game is inspired by Civilization 2 game. It was spotted on [the forum](<http://forum.lazarus.freepascal.org/index.php/topic,7914.0.html>) back in 2009. The engine used is not the original Civilization II engine. So as long as graphical resources are different, it's quite good TBS engine.
