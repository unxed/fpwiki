# List of books

From Lazarus wiki

Selected books covering programming in [Pascal](<Pascal.md> "Pascal") or [ObjectPascal](</index.php?title=ObjectPascal&action=edit&redlink=1> "ObjectPascal \(page does not exist\)") include: 

  * M. van Canneyt, M. Gärtner, S. Heinig, F. Monteiro de Cavalho, I. Ouedraogo: [Lazarus - The complete guide](<http://www.blaisepascal.eu/index.php?actie=./lazarus/lazarusreview>), Blaise Pascal Magazine 2011, Paperback, ISBN 978-94-90968-02-1 (English) 
  * M. van Canneyt, M. Gärtner, S. Heinig, F. Monteiro de Cavalho, I. Ouedraogo: [Lazarus - Klassenbibliothek und IDE](<http://www.cul.de/lazarus2.html>), CUL 2011, Paperback, ISBN 978-3-936546-63-7 (German) 
  * Michaël van Canneyt: [Free Pascal 2](<http://openlibrary.org/works/OL16799438W/>), CUL 2012, Paperback, ISBN 978-3-936546-72-9 (German) 
  * Howard Page-Clark: [Learn to Program using Lazarus](<http://openlibrary.org/works/OL16799439W>), Stichting Programmeertaal Pascal / Propascal Foundation 2013 (English) 



All of these books are focussed on development in Free Pascal and / or Lazarus.
