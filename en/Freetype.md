# Freetype

The original Freetype 1 font library had a version written in Pascal and compilable with old versions of FPC. This version was downloaded from <http://cvsweb.xfree86.org/cvsweb/xc/extras/FreeType/pascal/lib/Attic/> and brought back to life in the Lazarus-CCR. For other historical information see <http://www.freetype.org/freetype1/index.html>

## Download
    
    
     svn co <https://lazarus-ccr.svn.sourceforge.net/svnroot/lazarus-ccr/components/freetypepascal> freetypepascal
