## Commercial gain?

Why limit yourself to commercial gain? Why not replace "...Additionally, we make use of statistical services by Sourceforge and Berlios, as well as regular Apache logging. The data collected by these tools will not be distributed by us for commercial gain." with "Additionally, we make use of statistical services by Sourceforge and Berlios, as well as regular Apache logging. Unless required for legal reasons, any data that can be traced to individuals that is collected by these tools will not be distributed by us. We may however distribute aggregated, non-personnally identifiable information."
