,

The symbol `,` (pronounced "comma") is used in [Pascal](<Pascal.md> "Pascal") in several ways. 

  * It is used to separate the [variables](<Variable.md> "Variable").
  * It is used to separate the [constant](<Constant.md> "Constant") in [`case`](<Case.md> "Case") statement.
  * It is used to separate the [`unit`](<Unit.md> "Unit") in [`uses`](<Uses.md> "Uses") statement.



  


## other remarks

  * In [ASCII](<ASCII.md> "ASCII") the [character](<Char.md> "Char") `,` : has the value `44`.
