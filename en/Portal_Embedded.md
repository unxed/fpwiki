Portal:Embedded

[![Embedded Logo.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/d/da/Embedded_Logo.png)](</File:Embedded_Logo.png>) This portal provides an overview of development for Embedded platforms with Lazarus and Free Pascal. There are two types of embedded platforms: those with an [Operating System](<Glossary.md> "Glossary") and those without.
