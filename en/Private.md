# Private

│ **English (en)** │

  
Back to [Reserved words](<Reserved_words.md> "Reserved words"). 

  
The [modifier](<modifier.md> "modifier") `private` is part of [object-oriented programming](<object-oriented_programming.md> "object-oriented programming"). The modifier `private` designates within a [`class`](<Class.md> "Class") an area which only the [methods](<Method.md> "Method") of its own `class` can see. The modifier `private` means within a `class` an area that only the methods of its own `class` can access.
