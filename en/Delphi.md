**Delphi** is a commercial Rapid Application Development (RAD) tool for Windows, originally written by [Borland](<http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borland>). 

In Delphi, the included object library is known as the [Visual Component Library](<VCL.md> "VCL") (VCL). In the 2011 XE2 version, a second _cross-platform_ [GUI](<Graphical_User_Interface.md> "Graphical User Interface") library called [FireMonkey](</index.php?title=FireMonkey&action=edit&redlink=1> "FireMonkey \(page does not exist\)") was introduced. The Windows-only VCL also remains in the product.
