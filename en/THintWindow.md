# THintWindow

THintWindow it's a run-time (no design-time is provided) descendant of [TCustomForm](<TForm.md> "TForm") class, with a special support from Widgetset ([TWSHintWindow](</index.php?title=TWSHintWindow&action=edit&redlink=1> "TWSHintWindow \(page does not exist\)")). 

Special considerations: 

  * by default THintWindow
  * when shown WS prevents the HintWindow from acquiring focus. (TCustomForm are shown and are receiving focus right away)
