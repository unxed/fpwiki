# Free Pascal wiki:About

## Some guidelines to make this site manageable

Editing

  * Add some contact information or real name to your user page. You can access your user page by clicking on your login name at the top of the page.
  * Use the minor edit checkbox only when you make minor edits: fixing typos or formatting.
  * Comments, remarks and suggestions can be placed on the discussion pages (click on the discussion tab).
  * Don't edit other people's user pages, unless explicitly allowed.
