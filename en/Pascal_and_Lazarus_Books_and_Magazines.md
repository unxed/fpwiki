## Books about Pascal (or SQL) that are helpful, though not dealing specifically with Free Pascal or Lazarus

These books do not cover Lazarus or Free Pascal specifically but can still be very useful. 

  * [Essential Pascal](<http://www.marcocantu.com/epascal/>) \- Introduction to Pascal by Marco Cantu. Written originally in English but now translated into many languages.


  * [Object Pascal Handbook](<http://www.marcocantu.com/objectpascalhandbook/>) \- A new book by Marco Cantù, published August 2015.


  * [Essential Delphi](<http://www2.dcc.ufmg.br/disciplinas/pc/source/essential_delphi.pdf>) \- Delphi and Lazarus share many concepts and this book can help you understand Lazarus as well. Published April 2002. [Source code download](<https://www.marcocantu.com/edelphi/>) for the book.


  * [Computer Math and Games in Pascal](<https://www.blaisepascalmagazine.eu/product/books-computer-graphics-math-games-download-pdf/>) by David Dirkse. Published November 2015.


  * [Essential SQL](<http://www.marcocantu.com/edelphi/EssentialSQL_md6.pdf>) \- Useful if you use a SQL-based RDBMS such as Firebird, Interbase, PostgreSQL with FPC/Lazarus


  * [Getting Started with Firebird SQL Database](<https://www.amazon.co.uk/Getting-Started-Firebird-Learning-Doing/dp/0993272371/ref=sr_1_fkmrnull_3?crid=3KHQFVJ5WCYMZ&keywords=getting+started+with+firebird>) by Menkaura Abiola-Ellison. Published February 2019.


  * [Pascal User Manual and Report](<Pascal_User_Manual_and_Report.md> "Pascal User Manual and Report") ISO Pascal standard by Working Group 2 of the ISO committee lEC JTC I/SC22


  * [Delphi Basics](<http://www.delphibasics.co.uk/>) \- A booklike website that "provides help and reference for the fundamentals of the Delphi language. It gives an introduction to the Delphi Object Oriented Language for newcomers, and provides a ready reference for experienced programmers."


  * [Brinch Hansen on Pascal Compilers](<http://pascal.hansotten.com/per-brinch-hansen/>)



## Periodicals which publish content about Free Pascal and Lazarus

  * [Blaise Pascal Magazine](<http://www.blaisepascal.eu/>) \- A bi-monthly magazine dedicated to all types of Pascal programming, including [Lazarus](<Lazarus.md> "Lazarus")/[Free Pascal](<Free_Pascal.md> "Free Pascal"), [Delphi](<Delphi.md> "Delphi"). Offers English, Dutch and Russian editions.
  * [Clube Delphi](<http://www.clubedelphi.net/>) \- From Brazil in Portuguese. Mostly dedicated to Delphi, but also publishes Free Pascal/Lazarus material regularly.
  * [Pascal Gamer Magazine](<http://www.pascalgamer.com/>) \- a free, online, downloadable (PDF) publication devoted to game development in Pascal



## Periodic Magazines which publish content about other Pascal variants

  * [Entwickler Magazin](<https://kiosk.entwickler.de>) A German magazine on software development that occasionally includes articles on Delphi.



## Scientific journals, which publish articles on Pascal, Free Pascal or Lazarus

  * [Olympiads in Informatics](<https://www.mii.lt/svietimas/informatikos-olimpiados>). A refereed scholarly journal that is focused on research and practice of professionals who work in the field of teaching and learning informatics. ISSN 1822-7732
