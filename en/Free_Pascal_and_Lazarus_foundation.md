# Free Pascal and Lazarus foundation

The **Free Pascal and Lazarus foundation** is a non-profit organisation dedicated to the promotion and sponsoring of the open source projects [Free Pascal](<FPC.md> "FPC") and [Lazarus](<Lazarus_Faq.md> "Lazarus Faq"). It collects funds to sponsor development of additional features. 

The foundation attempts to raise funds and sponsors specific features in the Lazarus and Free Pascal projects. 

It has been established as a non-profit foundation in The Netherlands as "Stichting Programming Free Pascal & Lazarus Foundation." 

The foundation does not own the Free Pascal or Lazarus code base, and the inclusion of new features is always subject to the approval of the Free Pascal or Lazarus teams.
