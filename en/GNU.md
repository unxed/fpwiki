# GNU

[![](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/a/a7/unix_logo.png)](</File:unix_logo.png>)

[](</File:unix_logo.png> "Enlarge")

Unix logo

GNU stands for: “GNU is not UNIX”. It is the name of an [operating system](<Operating_System.md> "Operating System"). 

The name “GNU” is often used to identify projects that are under the auspices of the “GNU project”. All related projects are FLOSS. In the context of Pascal, the [GNU Pascal](<GNU_Pascal.md> "GNU Pascal") compiler (GPC) has to be mentioned. 
  *[FLOSS]: Free/Libre Open Source Software
