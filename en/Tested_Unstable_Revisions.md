# Tested Unstable Revisions

From Free Pascal wiki

This article lists revisions from the unstable branch of Free Pascal (2.1.1) that were tested and found to be working on some platforms. This is mainly useful for people interrested on features only available on 2.1.1, like internal linker, or support for many platforms, like Windows CE and Windows 64-bits. 

##  Tested Revisions 

**5080** \- win32, win64 and wince 

**5091** to **5114** \- unusable on win32, win64 and wince 

**5191** \- good on all targets 

**5356** \- does not compile under win32 (ppc1 does not stop compiling pp.pas [neither fails, nor succeeds]) 

**5370** \- good on wince crosscompiler, but not using lazarus 

**5431** \- good on all targets, except wince. 

**5723** \- good on wince crosscompiler,compile r10352 of lcl on wince(not fully tested application created with it)
