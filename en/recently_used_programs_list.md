# recently used programs list

To make your program appear in the Recently Used Programs List on the Windows Start Menu after being loaded at least once, your application must have a shortcut on the Desktop, in Quick Launch or in the Start Menu, etc. Windows only adds programs that have a shortcut. 

  
[![RecentlyUsedProgramsListScreenshot.png](https://wiki.freepascal.org/images/c/c1/RecentlyUsedProgramsListScreenshot.png)](</File:RecentlyUsedProgramsListScreenshot.png>)

  
To prevent your program appearing in the Recently Used Programs List on the Start Menu, simply don't create any shortcuts to your program.
