# Clascal

│ **English (en)** │  **[français (fr)](</Clascal/fr> "Clascal/fr")** │    
****

**Clascal** was the first working prototype of [Object Pascal](<Object_Pascal.md> "Object Pascal") developed in 1983 by the Personal Office Systems (POS) division of Apple Computer (later renamed to **The Lisa Division** , still later to **The 32-Bit Systems Division**). It was the first true object-oriented version of [Pascal](<Pascal.md> "Pascal"). 

Clascal was an extension of [Lisa Pascal](<Lisa_Pascal.md> "Lisa Pascal") and strongly inspired by Xerox PARC's release of Smalltalk-80 and the language [Modula](</index.php?title=Modula&action=edit&redlink=1> "Modula \(page does not exist\)"). In turn it laid the foundation for the development of Apple's Object Pascal, later referred to as [Mac Pascal](<Mac_Pascal.md> "Mac Pascal"), when _Object Pascal_ turned into a generic term.
