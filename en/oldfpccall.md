# oldfpccall

│ **English (en)** │

Back to [Reserved words](<Reserved_words.md> "Reserved words"). 

The **oldfpccall** modifier: 

  * belongs to the calling conventions of internal and external subroutines;
  * is **deprecated**.



Example: 
    
    
    function subTest : string; [oldfpccall];
      begin
        subTest := 'abc';
      end;
